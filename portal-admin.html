How approvals work

Teachers & Ambassadors: Click Approve. Their account unlocks immediately - they can sign in to their portal right away with the password they set during signup.

Every approval opens a pre-filled welcome email in your mail app, addressed to the person you just approved. Send it - the signup page promises they will hear from us.

Mentors: Approving a mentor only clears them to be matched. No student is connected until you create the match on the Mentor Matching page and the student's guardian has consented.

Rejections are also immediate - the user sees a "not approved" message when they try to sign in.

Message Review

Flagged messages between mentors and students, most serious first

Messages are flagged automatically when they contain contact details, secrecy language, a suggestion to meet in person, or a push to move off the platform. A flag is not an accusation - a student sharing their school email with their own mentor lands here too. Read the thread before acting. Flags raised on messages sent by the adult rank above the same flag from a student.
