What a mentor does

A mentor is an adult with a science or engineering background who helps your child think through their science fair project — choosing a topic, designing an experiment, understanding their results. They guide the work. They do not do it.

How we keep your child safe

Every mentor is reviewed and background-checked by FairGame before they are matched with any student.

All conversation happens inside the FairGame website — never by text message, personal email, or social media.

Every message is permanently recorded, and is read by your child's teacher and the FairGame team. Nothing between your child and their mentor is private. Messages cannot be edited or deleted by anyone.

The mentor is never given your child's email, phone number, address, or schedule. They see a first name, a grade, and a project.

What a mentor will never do

If any of these happen, tell us immediately.
  • Ask for a phone number, personal email, or social media account
  • Ask to meet your child anywhere alone, or offer them a ride
  • Ask your child to keep anything from you or their teacher
  • Contact your child outside the FairGame website
Our system automatically flags messages like these for review, but you and your child are the best safeguard. Reach us any time at fairgameinitiative@outlook.com.

You can change your mind

This permission lasts one school year. You can withdraw it at any time, for any reason, without explaining — keep this email and use the link at the bottom of this page. Messaging stops immediately when you do.

We keep the record of past messages even after you withdraw. That is deliberate: if there were ever a concern, deleting the record would make it impossible to look into.
