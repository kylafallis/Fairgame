# Science fair landscape research
## Tennessee, Michigan, Ohio, California
### Compiled August 31, 2026

---

## What this covers and what it cannot

Every fair below was checked against the organization's own website or an
official PDF on August 31, 2026. Anything that could not be confirmed is marked
as a lead rather than presented as fact.

**School-level fairs are not findable by research.** No public directory of
individual school science fairs exists in any of these four states. School fairs
are announced on a classroom newsletter or a building calendar, and they
disappear when the year ends. A scraper cannot find them. That gap is worth
naming plainly, because it is the layer FairGame is actually built to serve.
School fairs enter the database one of two ways: a teacher registers one through
the teacher portal, or a fair manager creates a plan and marks it public. Every
school fair in the system will be one FairGame helped put on.

**Ohio uses the word district differently.** The Ohio Academy of Science calls
its middle tier Regional Science Day, though some host institutions still print
District Science Day on their own pages. Both names point to the same tier
between a school fair and State Science Day.

**Timing note.** The 2026-27 cycle is opening right now. Some fairs have posted
2027 dates. Others have not updated since spring. That mix is normal for late
August and it is the reason a monthly recheck is worth automating.

## OHIO

Ohio has the cleanest structure of the four states. One academy runs the whole
ladder, publishes a single contact sheet, and uses one submission platform.

### State level

**Ohio State Science Day** is run by The Ohio Academy of Science and held at
Covelli Center on the Ohio State campus in Columbus. The 2026 event ran
Saturday, May 16. Check-in opened at 7:40 am, judging ran 9:00 am to 12:30 pm,
awards began at 2:45 pm, and the day closed at 4:45 pm. Students qualify through
Regional Science Day. The 2027 date was not posted as of August 31, 2026.

**Buckeye Science and Engineering Fair** is Ohio's ISEF affiliate, also run by
the academy. The 2026 fair was Saturday, March 7 at CAS, 2540 Olentangy River
Road, Columbus. Grades 9 through 12. Submissions closed at 5:00 pm on
February 4, 2026. Entry was $50 per student. Six projects advanced to Regeneron
ISEF.

Both events share one office: The Ohio Academy of Science, 5910 Wilcox Pl.,
Suite G, Dublin, OH 43016, 614-389-2182, info@ohiosci.org.

## TENNESSEE

Tennessee has no single statewide science fair organization. Regional fairs feed
ISEF directly, and each runs its own calendar. That makes outreach a fair-by-fair
effort here, and it means FairGame's directory fills a real gap for Tennessee
students who currently have no one place to look.

### Southern Appalachian Science and Engineering Fair, Knoxville

Host: The University of Tennessee, Knoxville. Serves a 23-county region.
Judging is March 23, 2027, in the Agriculture and Natural Resources Building at
2431 Joe Johnson Dr. The awards ceremony is 6:00 pm on March 30, 2027. Junior
division is grades 6 through 8, senior division grades 9 through 12. Teacher and
student registration opened September 1, 2026.

Office: 209 Student Services Building, Knoxville, TN 37996, 865-974-9493.
Judge information sits at sasef.utk.edu/information-for-judges/. The site hides
its email address behind an obfuscator, so pull it from the live page.

### Middle Tennessee Science and Engineering Fair, Nashville

Saturday, April 3, 2027, in Featheringill Hall at Vanderbilt University.
Registration opened August 15, 2026 and closes December 4, 2026. Students and
teachers register through MySciFair with code USTN06. Judges apply through a
Google Form linked from the site. Contact: info@mtsef.org, 615-460-6221. The
organization is a 501(c)(3), EIN 81-0790680.

Worth noting: the SASEF related links page lists MTSEF as hosted at Belmont
University. MTSEF's own site says Vanderbilt. The fair's own site wins.

### Cumberland Plateau Regional Science and Engineering Fair, Cookeville

68th annual, May 1, 2026, at Tennessee Tech University's Academic Wellness
Center, 1150 McGee Blvd. Run through the Millard Oakley STEM Center. Junior
division is grades 5 through 8, senior grades 9 through 12. Registration closed
April 19, 2026 at 11:59 pm. Check-in ran 7:30 to 9:00 am, morning judging 9:30
to noon and afternoon judging 1:30 to 3:00, both closed to the public. In 2025,
participants came from Putnam, DeKalb, White, Bledsoe, Fentress, Macon, and Smith
counties.

Contact: Christina Hatley, chatley@tntech.edu, 931-372-6531.

### Chattanooga Regional Science and Engineering Fair

Held in Maclellan Gymnasium at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga through
the STEM Education Department. The newest published dates are March 4 to 6, 2025,
so this listing is stale rather than wrong. Department office: Hunter Hall 107A,
Dept 4154, 651 McCallie Ave, 423-425-2164.

### Memphis and Shelby County

Named as a Tennessee ISEF affiliate on the SASEF related links page. No current
public site, date, or named contact was found. This one needs a phone call to
Memphis-Shelby County Schools.

## MICHIGAN

Michigan runs a state fair that regional fairs feed, similar to Ohio, but the
public information is thinner and more scattered.

### Science and Engineering Fair of Metropolitan Detroit

The largest fair in the state and the best documented. The 2026 event ran
March 17 through 20 at Huntington Place in Detroit. Setup Tuesday March 17,
9 am to 7 pm. Judging Wednesday March 18, 8 am to 5 pm. Public viewing March 19
and 20, 9 am to 7 pm.

Counties served: Lenawee, Livingston, Macomb, Monroe, Oakland, Washtenaw, and
Wayne. Elementary division is grades 3 through 5, junior 6 through 8, senior 9
through 12.

Deadlines for the 2026 cycle: school affiliation and SRC review December 1, 2025;
all entries due online February 20, 2026. Fees were $75 per school, reduced to
$25 if paid by December 1 alongside a volunteer commitment, plus $25 per project
billed to the school.

Co-directors: Kathy Kitzmann and Tim Fino. Phone 248-471-9900, fax 248-479-0383,
email SF2026@sefmd.org. That inbox is cycle-numbered, so expect it to change for
2027.

The elementary division matters for FairGame. It is the only fair in this
research that accepts third graders, which makes it a useful proof point when
talking to elementary principals.

### Southwest Michigan Science and Engineering Fair

Friday, March 20, 2026, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm, at Western Michigan University's
College of Engineering and Applied Sciences in Kalamazoo. Hosted through the
Kalamazoo Area Mathematics and Science Center. High school. Up to ten projects
advance to the state fair, and grand award winners are invited to ISEF. No
contact name or email is published on the site.

### Michigan Science and Engineering Fair

Confirmed to exist and to run on the zFairs platform at misef.zfairs.com.
Regional fairs advance students here. The contact page would not load on
August 31, 2026, so dates and staff names remain unconfirmed. This is the single
highest-value gap in the Michigan research and should be a phone call, not
another web search.

### Michigan leads

**Flint Regional Science and Engineering Fair** appears in event listings for
March 2026 but needs a primary source.

**Western Upper Peninsula Science Fair and STEM Festival** is hosted annually by
Michigan Technological University in Houghton.

**Eastern Upper Peninsula Regional Science and Engineering Fair** is run by the
Eastern Upper Peninsula Intermediate School District. The page exists at
eupschools.org and has not yet been read for dates or contacts.

## CALIFORNIA

California has the most fairs and the least reliable public information. County
fairs are strong and well organized. The state layer is where the confusion sits.

### California Science and Engineering Fair

Named the official state science fair in 1990. Held at California Lutheran
University after many years at the California Science Center in Los Angeles. The
74th fair ran in 2025 and drew roughly 900 students across 18 categories. General
address CSEF@usc.edu.

Handle this listing with care. The csef.usc.edu Current page still displayed 2024
content on August 31, 2026, and the affiliated fairs page did not render its
list. Confirm by email before pointing any student or judge there.

### Los Angeles County Science and Engineering Fair

76th annual, March 8 and 9, 2026, at Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles. Founded
1950. More than 1,000 students from over 150 schools across 41 categories. The
organization has moved from lascifair.org to lacsef.org. The new site has entry
paths for students, site coordinators, judges, volunteers, and sponsors, which
means judge signup is already built and FairGame should route rather than
duplicate.

### Greater San Diego Science and Engineering Fair

72nd annual, Wednesday March 18, 2026, at the Balboa Park Activity Center.
Established 1955. Open to San Diego and Imperial County students in grades 6
through 12. Student registration opened January 2, 2026 and payments were due
March 12, 2026. Mailing address P.O. Box 15547, San Diego, CA 92175. Runs on
zFairs. Judging information at gsdsef.org/information/judging-info with a
separate category judge resources page.

### Synopsys Silicon Valley Science and Technology Championship

Run by the Santa Clara Valley Science and Engineering Fair Association. March 3
and 4, 2027, with check-in March 3. This fair has published the most complete
forward calendar of any in this research:

- Applications open September 28, 2026
- SRC and IRB pre-approval November 13, 2026
- Category judge registration opens December 1, 2026
- Final application deadline for new participants January 8, 2027
- Last day to modify project details February 18, 2027 at 8:00 pm
- Abstract upload February 20, 2027 at 8:00 pm

Mailing address P.O. Box 307, Los Altos, CA 94023. Inquiries go through a web
form at science-fair.org/scvsefa/contact2/.

### Alameda County Science and Engineering Fair

2027 dates are set. Setup Friday March 12, 3 to 8 pm. Judging Saturday March 13,
9 am to 4 pm. Awards Saturday March 27, 1 to 5 pm in the Performing Arts Complex
at Chabot College. Affiliated with the Thermo Fisher Scientific Junior Innovators
Challenge, the state fair, and Regeneron ISEF. Judge signup at acsef.org/judges.

### Riverside County Science and Engineering Fair

Run by the Riverside County Office of Education, and the most completely
documented fair in this entire research. Setup and judging Friday February 26,
2027, 7 am to 5 pm. Public viewing and awards Saturday February 27, 9 am to
2 pm with awards at noon. Riverside Convention Center, 3637 Fifth St., Riverside.
Grades 4 through 12.

Calendar: program launch August 17, 2026; Intent to Participate September 2,
2026; registration materials February 1, 2027; projects due in zFairs
February 8, 2027.

Named contacts, all at rcoe.us:

- Yadira Chavelas, Administrator, ychavelas@rcoe.us, 951-826-6570
- Jonathan Moreno, Digital Marketing Communications Manager, jmoreno@rcoe.us, 951-826-6667
- Alyssa Juarez, Events Technician, ajuarez@rcoe.us, 951-826-6613

A county office of education running a fair is a different partner than a
university. They already have district relationships, a communications staff, and
a mailing list. Start California here.

### Sacramento Regional STEM Fair

Serves Amador, Butte, El Dorado, Placer, Plumas, Sacramento, San Joaquin, Shasta,
Solano, Sutter, Yolo, and Yuba counties. Grades 5 through 12, with competitive
divisions from grade 6. Students must be under 20 on May 1, 2027. Only students
from Amador, Butte, El Dorado, Placer, Plumas, Sacramento, Shasta, Sutter, Yolo,
and Yuba counties may advance to the state fair, a limit worth knowing before
promising a San Joaquin or Solano student a state pathway. Contact
sacstemfair@sacstemfair.org, 916-441-3150. EIN 91-2169205. Event dates were not
posted as of August 31, 2026.

### California leads

**Orange County Science and Engineering Fair** is confirmed as an Irvine-based
nonprofit. Its website returned no content on August 31, 2026.

**Santa Cruz County Science and Engineering Fair** appears in public reference
sources and needs a primary site check.

## What this means for outreach order

Ohio is the base and the contact sheet is already complete, so the eleven
regional coordinators are a single afternoon of email.

Riverside County is the strongest cold opening outside Ohio. Named staff, real
phone numbers, an office that serves an entire county's districts, and a fair
eighteen months out with a public calendar.

SASEF and MTSEF are the Tennessee entry points, and both are inside their
registration windows right now.

Michigan needs a phone call before it needs an email. The state fair contact page
is the missing piece, and SEFMD's co-directors are the people most likely to
provide it.
