Mentor Conduct Policy

Required before you can be connected to any student

You are working with minors. These rules exist to protect the student, and to protect you - a mentor who follows them can never have their conduct credibly questioned, because there is a complete record of it.

1. All contact stays inside FairGame

Use the messaging in this portal. Do not give a student your phone number, personal email, or social media, and do not ask for theirs. Do not connect with a student on any outside platform, even if they ask first.

2. Nothing you send is private

Every message is stored permanently and is readable by the FairGame team and the student's teacher. Messages cannot be edited or deleted. Write as though a parent is reading, because one may be.

3. Never meet a student alone

Any in-person meeting happens at the school, during school hours, with a teacher or parent present and with FairGame informed in advance. Never offer a student a ride. Never invite a student to your home or workplace without their guardian present.

4. No secrets

Never ask a student to keep anything from their parents, guardians, or teachers. If a student tells you something that suggests they are being harmed or are at risk, you must tell the FairGame team immediately. Do not promise to keep it confidential.

5. Mentor the work, not the person

Your role is the science fair project. Guide their thinking, ask questions, review their design - do not do the work for them. Keep conversation on the project. Do not discuss your personal life or ask about theirs beyond what the project requires.

6. Report anything that feels wrong

If a conversation drifts somewhere uncomfortable, or a student discloses something serious, stop and contact fairgameinitiative@outlook.com. Raising a concern is never held against you.
